## Add these before you rely on it wirelessly

- No bootloader key. Add `&bootloader` somewhere on a high layer, or every firmware update means physically double-tapping reset on each half.
- No Bluetooth keys — a `.vil` cannot express them. You want at least `&bt BT_SEL 0`, `&bt BT_SEL 1` and `&bt BT_CLR` (clears the pairing for the current profile; the usual fix when the halves stop talking), and `&out OUT_TOG` to flip between USB and BLE. The `#include`s are already in the keymap.
- Layer 1 has the most room: 7 unused positions (1, 2, 3, 4, 17, 25, 26). Good place for the keys above.

## Not converted by design

- **Vial key overrides** — ZMK's nearest equivalent is `mod-morph`, which
  has different semantics. Rewrite by hand if you use them.
- **`alt_repeat_key`** — ZMK's `&key_repeat` repeats the last keycode
  rather than an alternate one.
- **QMK tap-dance's tap-then-hold branch** — ZMK tap-dance counts taps only.
